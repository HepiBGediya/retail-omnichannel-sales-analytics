# Retail Omnichannel Sales Analytics

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data from an omnichannel perspective (Offline + Online)
to identify revenue drivers, return-related risks, and customer behavior differences.
The goal is to support **profit-focused business decisions** rather than surface-level metrics.

---

## 2. Business Objectives
- Compare Offline vs Online channel performance
- Identify profitability risks due to returns
- Evaluate customer value and repeat behavior
- Optimize product and channel strategy
- Provide actionable business recommendations

---

## 3. Dataset
- Source: Public retail transaction dataset
- Granularity: Line-item level (one row per product per invoice)
- Time period: Multiple years
- Channels: Derived using transaction behavior (bulk vs small orders)

---

## 4. Tools & Skills Used
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy)
- **Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering**
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
- **Business & KPI Analysis**
- **GitHub Project Structuring**

---

## 5. Project Workflow
1. Data Understanding  
2. Data Cleaning & Validation  
3. Channel Derivation (Offline vs Online)  
4. Channel-wise Revenue & Returns Analysis  
5. Customer & Product Behavior Analysis  
6. Time-based & Geographic Trends  
7. Business Insights & Recommendations  

---

## 6. Key Insights
- Offline channel is the **primary and most stable revenue driver**
- Online channel suffers from **extremely high return rates**, eroding profitability
- Offline customers are more **identifiable and repeat-oriented**
- Product performance varies significantly by channel
- Channel effectiveness differs across geographic markets

---

## 7. Business Recommendations
- Prioritize Offline channel for stable revenue growth
- Optimize Online channel instead of scaling volume
- Apply channel-specific product assortments
- Reduce online return risk through stricter controls
- Use market-specific channel strategies

---

## 8. Key Takeaway
A uniform omnichannel strategy is not optimal.
Offline sales provide stable profitability, while Online sales require focused
optimization to reduce return-related losses and improve customer value.
